# Privacy Policy: TheRedCapot Auto-Poster

Last updated: 10 October 2026

TheRedCapot Auto-Poster is a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel
[The Red Capot](https://www.youtube.com/@theredcapot) to publish the channel's own
videos, photos and stories to the channel's own Facebook Page ("Theredcapot"), and to
add comments from that Page under its own posts.

## What the app does

- Publishes content made by the channel owner to the owner's own Facebook Page.
- Reads the view counts of that Page's own posts, to see which videos people watch.

## Data we collect

The app is used only by the Page owner. It has no public sign-in and no other users.

- It does not collect, store or share personal data about people who view, like,
  comment on or follow the Page.
- It does not use cookies or tracking, and it does not sell or pass data to anyone.
- The only credential it holds is the owner's own Page access token, kept on the
  owner's own computer.

## Data deletion

Because the app stores no personal data about other people, there is nothing to
delete. If you have a question about this policy or about a post on the Page, send a
message to the Page at https://www.facebook.com/1399928679863338 or
leave a comment on the YouTube channel, and we will answer.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
